Campaign setup

Domain: Detected after login

1. Campaign

Create the base campaign. In this step only the campaign name and type are required.

2. Phone routing

For inbound campaigns select an unused NCC number. For outbound campaigns enter the caller ID that will be patched into the campaign.

3. Workflow

This creates a workflow and later attaches it to the campaign. The workflow will include the out-of-hours routing condition.

4. Business hours

Define the schedule and business event. The workflow condition will use this business event name.

5. Messages

Capture the in-hours and out-of-hours messages. They are shown in the execution log until the NCC message endpoint is provided.

6. Review and create

Review the values, then create the campaign, phone routing, workflow and business hours in NCC.
